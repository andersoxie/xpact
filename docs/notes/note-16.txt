FUZZING (reports/mass-xpact-issue-0072/logs/tools/)
- fuzzgen.py <seed list> <out> 20000 67: mutations of the W3C files <= 4 KB, deterministic.
- fuzzprobe.c: each document whole, 1-byte, 7-byte and seeded random pieces, with handlers;
  text concatenated in the digest so callback splits do not count as differences.
- fuzzrun.py: restarts the probe after a crash or hang and records the file.
- fuzzcmp.py: per engine against Expat; baseline -> ours changes judged by Expat.
- shrink.py: removes bytes while the probe still dies. 400-byte documents -> `</£`.
- exprobe.c + relink-map2.ps1 + mapstack.py: the stack of a crash without a trace, with names.
